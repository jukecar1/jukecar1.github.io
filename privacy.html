Video Pipeline - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

What this app is

Video Pipeline is a private tool used by one individual (its developer) to upload and schedule videos on YouTube channels, and to publish posts to Instagram and Facebook accounts, that the developer owns. It is not distributed to or used by the general public.

What information it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests access to the owner's own YouTube account through the YouTube Data API (scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube). It uses this access only to read basic channel details (channel name and subscriber count, to confirm the correct channel is selected), upload videos, edit video titles, descriptions and schedules, and manage playlists on the owner's own channels.

If the owner connects their own Instagram professional accounts and Facebook Pages through Meta's APIs, the app uses that access only to publish photos, carousels, and videos to those accounts and to read basic information about them (such as account name and publishing status) so the correct account is confirmed before posting. It does not read or collect information about other Instagram or Facebook users.

How information is stored and used

Sign-in and access tokens (Google and Meta) are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are sent only to Google's and Meta's servers to make the API requests described above. The app does not collect, store, sell, share, or transfer information about any other person, and it uses no advertising or third-party analytics.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Video Pipeline's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions) and, for Instagram and Facebook, from the Apps and Websites section of the account's settings. To ask that any stored token be deleted, email the contact address below.
